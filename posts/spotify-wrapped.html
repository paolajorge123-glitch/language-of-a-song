Spotify Wrapped: We Are Fluent in Music

5 min read

Music is the sole language in the world we are all fluent in. From streaming to touring, it's no surprise that the music industry is a multi-billion dollar entity. Part of its success could be linked to unique marketing that connects us with the tap of a button. Spotify Wrapped, established officially in 2016, generates a personalized breakdown of users' music selections throughout the year. Your listening habits turned into vibrant, informative graphics on an interactive story walk you down memory lane once again.

Every song has a story, and with the beat of the drum we've teleported back to the Halloween party at Robert's house. Spotify offers its users auditory time travel as it provides real-time streaming statistics, such as total minutes listened, top songs, and top artists. It reminds listeners that they played the same song 135 times — a quirky memory that otherwise would have been forgotten. Spotify knows that a person's ongoing use of their services will work to their favor to build brand engagement and eventually connect users to other users. This shareable content is compatible with major platforms such as Instagram, X, and Facebook, which oftentimes sparks conversations on a global scale. Community conversation further develops connection to the brand, creating a sense of comfort and tradition.

Year after year, this strategy has proven successful in connecting, informing, and reminding the public that their memories matter and are safely kept a few clicks away on Spotify.
