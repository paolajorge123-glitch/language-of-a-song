About Paola

(That's POW! to her friends 🎤)

🎓 5+ Years in Advertising
🏥 Medical & Entertainment Industries
🎵 Always Had a Knack for Music
✨ Eclectic, Creative, Passionate

Paola Jorge to the world, and Pao (pronounced as POW!) to her friends and family, is a marketing professional with an eclectic taste for all that is expressive, creative, and passionate.

She carries over 5 years of experience in advertising and building brands in the medical, spiritual, entertainment, and media industries — but somehow always had a knack for music. What does it take to create an album concept, design merchandise, nail the stage setup, plan touring, maintain a fanbase, and most importantly, how does it all flow with the rollout of one successful campaign?

There is something remarkable and universal in music — it speaks to the world as a whole. The same thing can be said for marketing. At least, that's what Paola believes.

"Language of A Song is a vessel that transports you to a more nostalgic time with its retro feel and bright colors; through this, she is able to provide insightful and organic posts about all things marketing as it relates to the music industry."

This is a safe space to immerse yourself in a plethora of topics — think of it as a new playlist you're navigating. You may even find yourself fluently singing along and dancing to the beats of branding sooner than you think.
